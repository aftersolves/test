About the Studio

We build technology that elevates human potential.

AfterSolves was founded on the belief that modern software should be accessible, scalable, and deeply integrated with AI. We started as a small collective of senior engineers and designers who were frustrated with the slow, bureaucratic nature of traditional agency models.

Today, we operate as an extension of your team. We strip away the fluff and focus purely on engineering excellence. Whether it's building a new SaaS platform from scratch, integrating complex AI workflows, or designing an intuitive user interface, our execution is precise and transparent.

Our mission is simple: To solve complex business challenges through intelligent engineering, allowing our clients to focus on what they do best—growing their business.

Core Values

  • Radical Transparency in process and pricing.
  • Engineering Excellence above all else.
  • AI-First approach to problem solving.

General Policy Eligible Refunds Partial Refunds Non-Refundable Items Refund Requests

1. General Policy

Due to the custom nature of digital services, all payments are generally non-refundable once work has commenced.

2. Eligible Refund Situations

Refunds may be considered if:

  • Services are canceled before work begins.
  • AfterSolves is unable to provide the purchased service.
  • Applicable consumer protection laws require a refund.

3. Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may be issued when:

  • A project is partially completed.
  • The client requests project termination.

Completed work, consultation time, development work, designs, and administrative costs may be deducted from any refund.

4. Installment Refunds

For installment plans:

  • Only undelivered portions may be eligible for refund.
  • Administrative and processing fees are non-refundable.

5. Non-Refundable Items

The following are generally non-refundable once purchased:

  • Domain registrations
  • Hosting services
  • Cloud services
  • Third-party software
  • API credits
  • Subscription fees
  • Completed development work
  • Completed design work
  • Consultation services

6. Refund Requests

Refund requests must be submitted in writing.

Requests should include:

  • Client name
  • Project reference
  • Reason for request
  • Supporting information

7. Review Period

Refund requests are typically reviewed within seven (7) business days.

8. Final Decision

Refund decisions are made at the sole discretion of AfterSolves, subject to applicable laws.

Approved refunds will be issued using the original payment method whenever possible.
